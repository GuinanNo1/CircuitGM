PackTrim Trim Circuit Tracks and Circuit Rhythm packs alpha 0.1

Load a pack saved from Novation Components. PackTrim looks through every project in it and saves a smaller copy that keeps all of those projects, plus every sample and patch they use, and leaves out the rest.

Alpha 0.1, for testing. Keep your original pack, and please report anything odd on GitHub.

1 Load a pack

You're in simple mode: PackTrim shows how many samples and patches are kept and the trimmed size. Switch on Advanced to also see every sample and patch slot, and which projects use each one.

You're in Advanced mode: every sample and patch slot is shown. Switch back to Simple at any time; the trimming is the same either way.

4 Save the trimmed pack

Every slot keeps its number, so the projects are copied byte for byte and still find their sounds. A left-out slot is saved the way Components saves an empty one: a blank name and no file. Open the trimmed pack in Components and send it to your Circuit as usual.
