PackTrim Trim Circuit Tracks and Circuit Rhythm packs coming soon

Load a pack saved from Novation Components and save a smaller copy, with only the samples and patches its projects use.

What it will do

  • Trim to every project: leave out the samples and patches no project uses. Every slot keeps its number, so the projects play exactly as before.
  • Trim to one project: keep a single project and just the sounds it uses.
  • Works on Circuit Tracks (.circuittrackspack) and Circuit Rhythm (.circuitrhythmpack) packs, in your browser, with nothing uploaded.

Until PackTrim has its own page, the same trimming is in Circuit2MIDI (step 2).
