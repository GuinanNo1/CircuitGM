RhythmGM General MIDI pack for the Circuit Rhythm coming soon

A General MIDI sample set for the Circuit Rhythm, built from GeneralUser GS, the same source as the TracksGM drums.

What it will be

The Circuit Rhythm has no synth engine, so every General MIDI instrument becomes a pitched sample, alongside drum kits, in the Rhythm's 128 sample slots. Which instruments, how many notes of each, and how the slots are shared between instruments and drums are still to be worked out.

Until then, TracksGM is the General MIDI pack for the Circuit Tracks, and Circuit2MIDI already reads Circuit Rhythm packs.
