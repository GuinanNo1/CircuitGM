TracksGM General MIDI pack for the Circuit Tracks

A Novation Components pack for the Circuit Tracks with 128 synth patches in General MIDI order and 64 drum samples in 8 kits. Every patch was voiced and loudness-matched on the hardware. It works as a general-purpose pack on its own, and it is the sound set MIDI2Circuit builds on.

Download and install

Download TracksGM TracksGM_final.circuittrackspack · 128 patches · 64 samples (64.9 s of the 196.6 s sample limit)
  1. Open Novation Components (web or desktop) with your Circuit Tracks connected.
  2. Open TracksGM_final.circuittrackspack in Components and send it to a spare pack slot on the Tracks. It replaces that slot's projects, patches and samples, so save anything you want to keep first.
  3. Load the pack on the Circuit Tracks (see Novation's Circuit Tracks user guide for the Packs view).

Patch slot numbers match General MIDI program numbers: slot 1 is Grand Piano, slot 128 is Gunshot. The pack has no projects; use MIDI2Circuit to turn MIDI files into projects that use it.

The macros

Every patch shares one macro layout, so the eight knobs do the same job whichever sound is loaded. Each patch loads with its macros at zero, exactly as designed.

MacroDoes
1Thicken (density and detune)
2Edge (virtual sync)
3Longer attack and release
4Filter envelope amount
5Cutoff (opens dark sounds, closes bright ones)
6Resonance
7Vibrato depth
8Chorus

A few patches swap macro 1 or 7 for something more useful: tremolo on Electric Piano 1, and wavetable morph on the vocal patches (Choir Aahs, Voice Oohs, Synth Voice, Voice Lead, Choir Pad, Halo Pad, FX Soundtrack, FX Goblins, FX Sci-fi).

How it was made

The patches are generated by code from short recipes, one per GM program, then corrected against measurements of the real Circuit Tracks: filter cutoff in Hz, envelope times in seconds and EQ in dB were all calibrated on the hardware, and every patch was recorded and loudness-matched. The drums were rendered from GeneralUser GS through FluidSynth and trimmed to fit the sample limit.

The full method, the build tools and the patch data are in the tracksgm folder on GitHub; start with PACK.md.
