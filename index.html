CircuitGM General MIDI for the Novation Circuit

Sound packs and browser tools that bring General MIDI songs to the Novation Circuit Tracks and Circuit Rhythm, and take your Circuit projects back out as MIDI. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

Sound packs

TracksGM Ready

Circuit Tracks pack

The General MIDI sound set for the Circuit Tracks: 128 synth patches in GM order, voiced and loudness-matched on the hardware, plus 64 drum samples in 8 kits. Use it on its own, or as the sounds MIDI2Circuit builds on.

About and patch list · Download · Code

RhythmGM beta

Circuit Rhythm packs

General MIDI for the Circuit Rhythm in two packs from GeneralUser GS: RhythmInstGM, all 128 GM instruments in GM order, and RhythmDrumGM, eight drum kits of 16 hits in the same order.

About and sample lists · Download RhythmInstGM · RhythmDrumGM · Code

Tools

MIDI2Circuit alpha 0.1

MIDI → Circuit Tracks

Turns a Standard MIDI file, or a project from a Circuit Tracks or Circuit Rhythm pack, into a Circuit Tracks pack. Choose the parts for each track, hear them, and download a pack for Novation Components.

Open · Help · Code

Circuit2MIDI alpha 0.1

Circuit Tracks / Rhythm → MIDI

Turns a project from a Circuit Tracks or Circuit Rhythm pack into a Standard MIDI file: scenes in order, pattern chains, timing, gates and velocities, with drum samples mapped to notes.

Open · Help · Code

PackTrim Coming soon

Circuit Tracks / Rhythm packs

Trims a Circuit Tracks or Circuit Rhythm pack down to the samples and patches its projects use, or to a single project, and saves the smaller pack.
