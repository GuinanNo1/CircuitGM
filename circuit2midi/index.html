Circuit2MIDI Circuit Tracks and Rhythm to MIDI converter alpha 0.1

Quickstart · User guide

Open a Novation Components pack for the Circuit Tracks or Circuit Rhythm, trim it by removing the samples and patches no project uses, and turn one of its projects into a Standard MIDI file: every scene, pattern chain and step, on the channels and sounds you choose.

Alpha 0.1, for testing. The Circuit Rhythm project format was worked out from one hardware-saved project, so check the result against your Circuit, keep your original pack, and please report anything odd on GitHub.

1 Load a pack

You're in simple mode: the main choices, with settings that suit most projects. Switch on Advanced to trim the pack, and for detailed control over scene order, swing, chance steps, the MIDI channels and instruments, and drum notes. Tuning those settings can give a closer match to your Circuit, but may take some experimenting.

You're in Advanced mode: every setting is shown. Switch back to Simple at any time; your settings are kept and still applied.

2 Trim the pack optional

What would be removed

Samples and patches no project uses are left out. Every slot keeps its number, so the projects are copied unchanged and still find their sounds. A sample a project picks per step counts as used, and so does each track's default sample. Keep: only project N also empties the other project slots and keeps just that project's sounds; the project stays in its own slot.

3 Choose a project

One project is converted at a time. Empty project slots aren't listed.

Opens the chosen project in MIDI2Circuit to build a Circuit Tracks pack from it, with the pack's own samples (and, from a Circuit Tracks pack, its patches). The pack is handed over inside your browser; nothing is uploaded.

4 Check the scenes

0:00 The preview plays the pack's own samples; Circuit Tracks synths are sketched with simple tones.

Each column is a scene, in playing order; the numbers are the pattern chain each track plays in it. A scene lasts as long as its longest chain, and shorter chains repeat. Click a dot to leave a track out of the preview and the MIDI file. S solos a track in the preview only.
