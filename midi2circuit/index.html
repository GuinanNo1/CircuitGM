MIDI2Circuit MIDI to Circuit Tracks converter alpha 0.1

Quickstart · User guide

Turn a General MIDI file, or a project from a Circuit Tracks or Circuit Rhythm pack, into a Novation Circuit Tracks pack. Pick the parts you want, hear how they'll play, and download a .circuittrackspack for Novation Components, built on the TracksGM pack or a pack of your own.

Alpha 0.1, for testing. Packs load and play on the Circuit Tracks, but the project format is reverse-engineered: keep a backup of your packs, and please report anything odd on GitHub.

1 Load a MIDI file or Circuit pack

You're in simple mode: the main choices, with settings that suit most songs. Switch on Advanced for detailed control over how the song is laid out, the base pack, trimming and warnings. Tuning those settings can give better, more compact results, but may take some experimenting.

You're in Advanced mode: every setting is shown. Switch back to Simple at any time; your settings are kept and still applied.

2 Choose the parts

Give each part a Circuit Tracks track. You can send several MIDI parts to the same track to combine them. Synth 1 and Synth 2 play on the Tracks itself; MIDI 1 and MIDI 2 play external gear; Drums uses the GM drum part (channel 10).

These send notes on the MIDI channels set on your Circuit Tracks (Setup view), to play external gear.

4 Check the arrangement

    0:00 The preview plays the converted parts: quantised to the step grid, 6 notes per step, one drum sample per step.

    Click a coloured dot to mute or unmute a whole row, or a box to mute or unmute those bars. Mutes change the preview and the pack. The numbers are the pattern each Circuit Tracks track plays; the rows under it are the MIDI parts feeding it. S solos a track in the preview only.

        5 Build the pack

        Open the pack in Novation Components and send it to your Circuit Tracks. It holds the whole base pack (TracksGM pack: 128 patches, 64 drum samples) plus the new projects in project slots 1 onwards, so it replaces whatever pack you send it over. In each project, scenes 1 onwards hold the song's sections in order and are saved as one scene chain.
