PatchWorker help

PatchWorker edits the synth patches of a Novation Circuit Tracks. It opens with the TracksGM pack. You can work with no hardware (the page plays the patch itself) or link a Circuit Tracks and hear every edit on the real thing. Nothing is uploaded: files stay on your computer.

Quick start

  1. Pick a patch from the list on the left.
  2. Play it: click the keys along the bottom, type on the A row of your keyboard (W E T Y U are the black keys, Z / X change octave), or press Space to run a loop while you edit. Keys S / M / L in the bottom bar sets the keyboard's size.
  3. Turn knobs: drag up or down. Hold Shift for fine steps, scroll over a knob, or use the arrow keys. Double-click a knob to reset it. Click the number under a knob to type a value.
  4. Download pack saves the whole pack with your edits, ready to send to the Circuit Tracks with Novation Components. Patch ▾ → Download .syx saves just the current patch.

Edits go straight into the pack in your browser: there is no separate Save step. An orange dot marks every patch changed since the pack was opened. Undo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) works per patch, Compare (hold it) plays the patch as the pack had it, and Patch ▾ → Revert puts it back. Your session is kept in the browser, so closing the tab does not lose it.

The three pages

The pages and controls follow Novation Components' Synth editor.

MainOscillators 1 and 2 (wave, Index / pulse width, Interpolate, VSync, Cents, Semitones, Density, Detune, pitch-bend range), Mixer, Filter (type, Bypass routing, Frequency, Resonance, Q, Key Track, Env 2 > Freq, Drive and drive type), Envelopes 1 Amp / 2 Filter / 3 Mod, and LFOs 1 and 2.
EffectsChorus / Phaser, three-band Equaliser, Distortion, and Voice (Mono, Auto-Glide or Poly, Portamento, Pre-Glide, Keyboard Octave).
ModulationWhat the selected macro controls (up to four destinations, each with Start, End and Depth), and the 20-row mod matrix.

The eight Macros sit above every page. Their positions are saved in the patch. Click a macro to select it; the Modulation page then shows its destinations. The line under each knob names what it controls.

Values shown with ~ (Hz, ms, dB) are measured from a real Circuit Tracks, so "Frequency ~650 Hz" is what the hardware does at that setting. A Sync box swaps a rate knob to tempo-locked note lengths.

Linking a Circuit Tracks

  1. Connect the Circuit Tracks by USB and close Novation Components (only one program can use the SysEx connection reliably).
  2. Use Chrome, Edge or another browser with Web MIDI. Click Link Circuit Tracks and allow MIDI access when the browser asks.
  3. Choose Synth 1 or Synth 2. The current patch is sent to that synth, and from then on, with Live ticked, every edit goes to the hardware as you make it. Choosing another patch in the list sends it too.
SendSends the current patch to the chosen synth now. This loads it as that synth's working patch; it does not overwrite anything stored on the Circuit Tracks.
GetFetches the patch the chosen synth is playing, so you can keep a sound you made on the hardware. You choose which pack slot it goes into.
Store…Writes the current patch into one of the 128 patch slots on the Circuit Tracks itself, replacing what is there. PatchWorker asks first.
Hear onBrowser, Tracks or Both. Linking switches to Tracks, so notes from the page play the hardware.

Turning a macro knob on the Circuit Tracks moves the same macro on the page. A second MIDI keyboard plugged into the computer plays the patch as well. On the Circuit Tracks, MIDI note and control receive must be on in Setup view.

To put a whole edited pack on the Circuit Tracks, download it here and send it with Novation Components.

Files

Open pack…Opens a .circuittrackspack from Components. Samples and projects are kept exactly as they are.
Download packSaves the pack with your patch edits and its name (up to 16 characters). If a project in the pack plays a patch you changed, PatchWorker offers to update that project's own copy of the patch too: projects carry their own copy of each synth patch, and only copies that still matched the pack are touched.
Open .syx…Loads one patch, or a bank of many, into the slots you choose. Patches from the original Circuit load too. You can also drop .syx and pack files anywhere on the page.
Download .syxThe current patch as a 350-byte SysEx file.
Bank .syxAll 128 patches in one file.
Load TracksGMClears the session and starts again from the TracksGM pack.

How close is the browser sound?

The browser synth is an approximation, built on the Web Audio synth from circuit-tracks-tools by Namir Sayed-Ahmad Baraza, with its parameter curves replaced by CircuitGM's measurements of a real Circuit Tracks (filter cutoff, envelope times, LFO rate, modulation depth to pitch and filter, EQ, and Pre / Post FX level). It is good for shaping a sound; the hardware has the last word. Known differences:

  • The wavetable waves (Sine Table to Random Collection) are stand-in shapes. The Sine Table and the pulse and blend tables follow the measured behaviour; the vocal and random tables only resemble it.
  • Controls marked with a small orange ring (Q, pitch-bend range, LFO Slew) change the patch but not the browser sound. The same goes for Common Sync, and for mod matrix rows aimed at V-Sync, Pulse Width, Drive, LFO Rate and envelope Decay.
  • Keyboard Octave sets the octave the Circuit Tracks' own pads and patch preview start in. It does not shift notes played from this page or over MIDI, on the hardware or in the browser.
  • Distortion, filter drive, chorus and phaser are simpler than the hardware's.
  • How far a macro moves its destination is not yet measured on hardware, so macro ranges are estimates.

Every byte of a patch is kept, including anything PatchWorker does not show, so a patch saved here is exactly what you edited plus what was already there.

If something is not working

  • No sound in the browser: click a key once (browsers only start audio after a click), and check Volume and that Hear on is Browser or Both.
  • "No Circuit Tracks found": check the USB cable, close Components, then click Look again. Safari has no Web MIDI.
  • The Circuit Tracks does not play notes from the page: switch on MIDI note receive in its Setup view.
