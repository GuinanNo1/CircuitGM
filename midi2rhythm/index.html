MIDI2Rhythm MIDI to Circuit Rhythm converter For testing

How it works

Turn a General MIDI file into a Novation Circuit Rhythm pack. Every Rhythm track plays one note at a time, so MIDI2Rhythm picks the parts that matter most (drums, bass, melody, then the busiest), turns chords into a single line, keeps each line near its sample's own pitch, and builds a .circuitrhythmpack on RhythmInstGM with a RhythmDrumGM kit in slots 113–128.

For testing: not yet tried on a Circuit Rhythm. The project format is reverse-engineered, so keep a backup of your packs, and please report anything odd on GitHub.

1 Load a MIDI file

You're in simple mode: MIDI2Rhythm chooses the parts, turns chords into one line with Smart line, and lays out the song. Switch on Advanced to pick how each part is reduced, its sound, the range fitting and the arrangement settings.

2 Choose the parts

Each part is labelled by how many notes it plays at once. Mono and mostly mono parts (only overlapping note tails) go across as they are. Chords are split into separate voices on free tracks, or turned into one line when there's no room. Untick a part to leave it out and free its track for another.

"Chords become" sets how each chord part is handled. Auto splits it into its separate voices, one track each with the same sound, when there are free tracks, and otherwise turns it into one line with Smart line.

    3 Sounds and fitting

    Each RhythmInstGM sample is one recording at one pitch (its note is in its name, e.g. "001 Grand Piano C4"). Played far from that note it speeds up or slows down and stops sounding like the instrument, so each part is moved by whole octaves towards it, and any stray notes are folded an octave towards it. RhythmInstGM's GM programs 113–128 make way for the drum kit: Tinkle Bell, Agogo, Steel Drums, Woodblock, Taiko, Melodic Tom and Synth Drum play on a similar instrument; the sound effects are left out.

    4 Check the arrangement

    0:00 The preview plays the RhythmGM samples as the Rhythm will: one note per step per track, each new note cutting off the last.

    Click a coloured dot to mute or unmute a whole track, or a box to mute or unmute those bars; mutes change the preview and the pack. The numbers are the pattern each Rhythm track plays; the rows under it are the MIDI parts feeding it (their dot leaves the part out, so its track goes to another part). S solos a track in the preview only.

        5 Build the pack

        Open the pack in Novation Components and send it to your Circuit Rhythm. It holds RhythmInstGM's instruments in slots 1–112, the chosen drum kit in 113–128, and the song in project slots 1 onwards, so it replaces whatever pack you send it over. In each project, scenes 1 onwards hold the song's sections in order and are saved as one scene chain.
