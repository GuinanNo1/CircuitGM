MIDI2Rhythm MIDI to Circuit Rhythm converter For testing · v0.6

How it works

Turn a General MIDI file, or a project from a Circuit Tracks or Circuit Rhythm pack, into a Novation Circuit Rhythm pack. Every Rhythm track plays one note at a time, so MIDI2Rhythm picks the parts that matter most (drums, bass, melody, then the busiest), turns chords into a single line, and builds a .circuitrhythmpack on RhythmInstGM with a RhythmDrumGM kit in slots 113–128.

For testing: not yet tried on a Circuit Rhythm. The project format is reverse-engineered, so keep a backup of your packs, and please report anything odd on GitHub.

2 Choose the parts

The project's scenes, chains and patterns are copied as they are. Drum 1–4 go to tracks 5–8 with their samples; the synths (then MIDI 1 and 2, if there's room) take the other tracks from track 1, one track per voice. Voices sets how many notes per step a synth keeps: Auto gives it as many as it plays at once, while the tracks last.

Each part is labelled by how many notes it plays at once. Mono and mostly mono parts (only overlapping note tails) go across as they are. Chords are split into separate voices on free tracks, or turned into one line when there's no room. Untick a part to leave it out and free its track for another.

"Chords become" sets how each chord part is handled. Auto splits it into its separate voices, one track each with the same sound, when there are free tracks, and otherwise turns it into one line with Smart line.

    3 Sounds and fitting

    Each RhythmInstGM sample is one recording at one pitch (its note is in its name, e.g. "001 Grand Piano C4"). Played far from that note it speeds up or slows down and can stop sounding like the instrument. Notes play at their written pitch by default; in Advanced, Keep lines near the sample's note moves each part by whole octaves towards it and folds stray notes an octave towards it, for songs that need it. RhythmInstGM's GM programs 113–128 make way for the drum kit: Tinkle Bell, Agogo, Steel Drums, Woodblock, Taiko, Melodic Tom and Synth Drum play on a similar instrument; the sound effects are left out.

        6 Build the pack

        Open the pack in Novation Components and send it to your Circuit Rhythm. It holds RhythmInstGM's instruments in slots 1–112, the chosen drum kit in 113–128, and the song in project slots 1 onwards, so it replaces whatever pack you send it over. In each project, scenes 1 onwards hold the song's sections in order and are saved as one scene chain. Components doesn't take a pack's colour from the file, so set it there after importing.
